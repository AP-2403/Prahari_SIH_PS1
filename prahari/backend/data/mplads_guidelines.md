# MPLADS Condensed Guidelines — For PRAHARI AI Assistant RAG Context

## Overview
MPLADS (Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme) allows each MP to recommend works worth ₹5 crore per year for local infrastructure development.

## §2.1 — Permissible Works
Works must be:
- Of durable nature for creation of assets
- In the public interest
- Located within the MP's constituency (Lok Sabha) or state (Rajya Sabha)

**Ineligible expenditure:**
- Land acquisition
- Religious structures (temples, mosques, churches)
- Private property/private land
- Monuments, statues, and memorials
- Political offices or party offices

## §6.2 — Individual Work Limits
- No work should be split artificially into smaller sub-works to circumvent approval limits
- Each individual work recommendation must be for a contiguous, complete work

## §7.4 — Time Limit for Completion
- All sanctioned works must be completed within **1 year** of sanction
- Works not completed within 3 years are liable for cancellation
- Implementing agencies must submit utilization certificates within 3 months of completion

## §7.6 — Photographic Documentation
- Implementing agencies must submit before/after photographs
- Completed works with no photographic evidence are non-compliant
- Photos must show the actual work site (not generic or duplicate images)

## §8.2 — Funding through Trusts/Societies
- Maximum ₹1 crore (₹10,00,000) can be sanctioned to any single trust, society, or NGO per year
- Trusts must be registered and audited
- Accounts must be audited within 6 months of year-end

## §10.1 — SC/ST Area Earmarking
- Each MP must earmark minimum **15%** of their annual entitlement for works in SC (Scheduled Caste) areas
- Each MP must earmark minimum **7.5%** for works in ST (Scheduled Tribe) areas
- These are cumulative minimums, not per-work requirements
- Failure to meet these thresholds by year-end results in a compliance flag

## §12 — Role of District Authority (DA)
- District Collector/DM is the nodal implementing authority
- DA must inspect works and certify completion
- DA must ensure no duplicate works are sanctioned
- DA certifies that vendor selection followed procurement rules

## §13 — Vendor/Implementing Agency Selection
- Works above ₹10 lakh must follow competitive tendering
- Single-source procurement must be documented and justified
- Same vendor cannot hold >40% of district MPLADS spend (recommended guideline)
- Conflict-of-interest declarations required from all vendors

## §15 — Audit and Verification
- Annual audit by Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG)
- Works must reconcile with DIC (District Implementation Committee) records
- Photo evidence is mandatory for CAG certification
- Any discrepancy between sanctioned and expenditure amounts >5% requires explanation

## Utilization & Payment
- Funds must be utilized within 3 years or lapse
- Balance unspent at MP term-end reverts to government
- Vendor payment must be made within 45 days of work completion
- Delayed vendor payments are flagged as "Balance Not Yet Paid"
